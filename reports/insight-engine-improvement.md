## Analysis guardrails

- A cross-Agent pattern requires supporting findings from at least two distinct Agents.
- One comparable absence moves a pattern to Watching; two consecutive comparable absences move it to
  Resolved. Missing coverage and INCOMPLETE runs do not advance the count.
- Only `insight_engine` ownership can support a pattern or recommendation.
- Every future pattern and recommendation must link to its per-Agent evaluations.
- Raw prompts, responses, traces, provider IDs, private resource identifiers, and customer data are
  forbidden.
- Recommendations must not encode Test Agent issue IDs, fixed prompts, or known answers into production
  behavior.
